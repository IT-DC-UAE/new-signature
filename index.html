Manual: how to use your signature

Step 1: Add your details

  1. Type your full name, job title, phone number and email in the boxes above.
  2. Check the preview. It must show your own details, not the sample text.

Step 2: Add the signature

First click Copy signature, then follow the method for your email. Not sure which Outlook you have? If you see a "Try the new Outlook" switch at the top right, you are on classic Outlook. If not, use Method 2.

Method 1: Outlook (classic)

  1. In Outlook go to File > Options > Mail > Signatures > New.
  2. Give it a name, click in the box and press Ctrl + V to paste.
  3. Choose it as the default for new messages and replies, then click OK.

Method 2: New Outlook

  1. Click the gear icon (Settings) > Accounts > Signatures.
  2. Click New signature, give it a name, click in the box and press Ctrl + V to paste.
  3. Choose it as the default for new messages and replies, then click Save.

Method 3: Gmail

  1. Click the gear icon > See all settings > General > Signature > Create new.
  2. Give it a name, click in the box and press Ctrl + V to paste.
  3. Choose it for new emails and replies, then click Save Changes.

Step 3: Test

Check that your name, number and email are correct, the links are black and the banner shows.
